FireAndIceClock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FireAndIceClock handles information when you use the FireAndIceClock mobile app.

Information Collection

FireAndIceClock does not require an account or registration. The app uses your device's date and time settings to display the current time and date.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer, provided that no third-party services or other components included in the app do so.

Location Information

FireAndIceClock does not need to access your device's precise location to display the time and date. The app uses your device's configured time and time zone settings.

Data Sharing

The app does not sell or share personal information collected by the app with third parties. This statement does not cover data collection by Apple or other services outside the app's control.

Data Security and Retention

Because the app is designed to display a clock and visual themes without collecting personal information, it does not maintain a developer-operated database of user information.

Children's Privacy

FireAndIceClock does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app or its privacy practices change. Updates will be published on this page, along with a revised update date when applicable.
