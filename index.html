🔥❄️ FireAndIceClock Support

Welcome to the official support page for FireAndIceClock, a digital clock featuring a live time display, current date, and animated FIRE and ICE themes.

How to Use the App

  • Open FireAndIceClock to see the current time and date.
  • Tap FIRE to switch to the animated fire theme.
  • Tap ICE to switch to the animated ice theme.
  • The clock uses your device's date, time, and time zone settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the displayed time incorrect?
Check your device's date, time, and time zone settings. You can enable automatic time settings in your device settings.

Why is the animation not running smoothly?
Animation performance can vary by device. Try closing and reopening the app.

Do I need an account to use FireAndIceClock?
No account or sign-in is required.
